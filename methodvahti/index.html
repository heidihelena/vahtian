What MethodVahti does not decide

It does not certify that your sample is adequate, declare saturation, infer causality, or judge the quality of the study. The modelled range is decision support. You decide the sample and defend the reasoning.

The current heterogeneity model is a Vahtian hypothesis and is not yet externally validated. The assumptions, coefficients, and validation plan are open.
