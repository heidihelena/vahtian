Privacy

Last updated 20 June 2026.

The short version. vahtian.com has no accounts and no advertising trackers. The interactive demos run entirely in your browser. The downloadable tools are local-first — your manuscripts and ratings stay on your device.

Who we are

Vahtian (Heidi Andersén), Vaasa, Finland, is the data controller for this website. Questions or requests: privacy@vahtian.com.

What this page covers

This notice is about the website (vahtian.com) and the demos shown on it. Each downloadable tool has its own behavior — see, for CiteVahti, the contributor privacy notice (the optional, default-off evidence-contribution feature).

What we collect

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no advertising cookies.
  • Cookieless analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records aggregate metrics (page views, referrers, approximate country) without cookies and without building a personal profile or tracking you across other sites.
  • Technical logs. Our host (Cloudflare) processes standard request data, including IP address, to deliver and protect the site. We do not use it to identify you.
  • The on-page demos (e.g. CiteVahti's "check one claim") run entirely in your browser. Anything you type or paste is not sent to us or stored.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not run advertising/retargeting pixels or cross-site trackers.
  • We do not require an account to use any free tool.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, or portability of any personal data we hold, and object to processing. Because the site is cookieless and account-free, we usually hold no data that identifies you. Contact privacy@vahtian.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Changes

If this notice changes — for example if we add a feature that processes personal data — we will update this page and the date above.
