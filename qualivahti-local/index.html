How it stays honest

Models suggest structure. You decide interpretation. Local AI transcribes, proposes codes with rationale and marked uncertainty, clusters, and argues against your themes on request. Not one of its words becomes analysis until you accept, edit, or reject it.

The second opinion is deliberately a second model — a different one than the coder, because agreement from the same reader is not a second opinion. Frequency charts carry the words "frequency is not importance" on the chart itself. The evidence map shows grounding, never a confidence score.

Common questions

Does my data touch any server?

No. The only network address in the scripts is localhost — your machine talking to itself. Setup downloads are the only fetches, and no research data is involved in them.

My interviews are in Finnish or Swedish.

Whisper handles both, and about 100 more languages. For dialects and mixed-language interviews, expect to correct more — our own honest error-rate account is in the free transcription guide. The workflow assumes correction; that is the method, not a flaw.

Is this an NVivo replacement?

It is an NVivo alternative for studies where the data was never allowed to reach a cloud tool anyway. It is a method-disciplined workflow, not a feature checklist, and it costs one payment instead of a yearly subscription.

What exactly is the AI allowed to do?

Transcribe (you correct), suggest codes (you review every row), cluster codes into candidate groupings (you decide themes), argue against your themes (you weigh it), and draft scaffolds from your facts (you write the meaning). Every model run is logged with name and version.

My journal requires the analysis code with submission.

Covered. The analysis scripts are open source (Apache-2.0) with a citable version, so you can include them in supplements and repositories freely. The vault ships a how-to-cite guide with ready methods sentences, software citations, and code/data availability statements a reviewer will accept.

What it is not

  • Not a theme generator, and not a claim that your codes are true — it records what you decided and why, which is what can be defended.
  • Not a SaaS. You own the files, permanently.
  • Not a medical device, and not clinical or ethics advice.

If you only need transcription, the free guide is enough. If you also need coding, a codebook, audit trails, evidence maps, and drafts — QualiVahti Local packages the complete workflow.
