Questions

Does research data touch a server?

The included scripts use local files and localhost to talk to Ollama on your machine. App and model downloads use the internet during setup, without research data. Your own operating-system services and backup settings remain your responsibility.

Does it work with Finnish or Swedish interviews?

Yes. Whisper supports both and the guide explains language selection. You still correct names, specialist terms, dialect, mixed-language speech, and speaker labels against the recording.

Is this an NVivo replacement?

It is a local, file-based workflow for transcription, coding review, codebooks, memos, themes, evidence maps, and reporting. It is not a feature-for-feature clone and does not hide the files in a proprietary project format.

Can a doctoral school or lab use it?

The EUR 49 licence is personal. Lab, teaching, and doctoral-school licences are available from hello@vahtian.com.
