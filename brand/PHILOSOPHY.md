# Sentinel Geometry — the Vahtian mark system

**Philosophy:** restraint as trust. A citation-integrity company cannot look
clever; it must look *certain*. The marks are built from one idea — **the bracket
as a gate** — repeated with the patience of a typesetter and the discipline of a
proof.

**Form & space.** Every mark lives inside the same quiet architecture: a navy
tile, generous interior air, and a single pair of brackets `[ ]` holding one small
glyph at the optical centre. The brackets are the constant — the gate every claim
must pass through. What sits between them is the only thing that changes. Nothing
floats; nothing decorates. The negative space is not empty, it is *withheld* —
the visual equivalent of "not yet verified."

**Color & material.** Deep navy (#2D2440) as the ground of seriousness. The
brackets carry the family's four hues — amber, teal, violet, rose — never green,
never alarm-red, because support is a measurement, not a verdict. The inner glyph
is always a calm lilac, so the eye reads *frame, then content* — process before
conclusion. Color distinguishes the siblings, but never alone: each glyph is a
different *shape*, legible in a single ink.

**Scale & rhythm.** Drawn on a 32-unit grid, ~2px strokes, rounded caps — sized to
survive at 24px in a browser tab and still feel deliberate at billboard size. The
rhythm is the bracket's two short serifs, top and bottom, repeated across all five
marks like a recurring cadence.

**Hierarchy.** The parent (Vahtian) holds a shield — the sentinel itself. The
products hold what they verify: two dots (a claim), an eye (review), a check (a
standard), a constellation (an atlas of evidence). Siblings at a glance, distinct
on inspection.

**Craft.** These are not generated; they are *adjusted* — every serif length,
every dot radius, every curve tension settled by eye until the set feels
inevitable. The work should read as the product of painstaking attention, the kind
that only looks simple because someone laboured over every unit until nothing
remained to remove.
