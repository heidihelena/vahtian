Pre-Submission Check

Treat your manuscript like code: run the checks in order, fix the gaps, keep the report.

Every check on this page runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. A machine can see whether a statement is present or a reference resolves. Whether each is adequate, and whether the manuscript is ready, is your judgment. A clean run is not a clearance.

The check chain

  1. Statements & structure scan: ethics, consent, funding, competing interests, data availability, registration, AI disclosure; section headings.Runs on this page, step 1 below.
  2. Reporting-guideline walk: STROBE, CONSORT or PRISMA, item by item, marked addressed / gap / not applicable.Runs on this page, step 2 below.
  3. Reference check: DOIs against Crossref and DataCite for mismatches, retractions, duplicates.Reference check (free, in the browser).
  4. Claim-wording check: uncited claims, overclaiming, vague evidence language.Citation quick check · Pattern mirror (free, in the browser).
  5. Claim–source support: does each cited source support the exact claim it is attached to?CiteVahti, a free local audit. Human first, AI second.
  6. Report: what was checked, what is open, what a machine could not resolve. The sign-off line is yours.Builds as you go, step 6 below.

Step 1 · runs here

Statements & structure scan

No account · no upload · nothing leaves your browser.

What "found" means. The scan matches wording, nothing more. "Found" means a sentence looks like the statement: read it and judge whether it says what your journal requires. "Not found" means the usual wording is absent; yours may be phrased differently, or it may be missing. Requirements vary by journal, so check the author instructions of your target journal.

Step 2 · runs here

Reporting-guideline walk

Pick the guideline that matches your study design, then mark each item. Progress is saved in your browser only. Item labels are abbreviated; the official checklist is the authority: STROBE · CONSORT · PRISMA 2020. Unsure which applies? The EQUATOR Network lists guidelines by study type.

Steps 3 to 5 · run the tools, mark them here

References and claims

These checks have their own free tools. Run each, then mark it done here so your report records it. The marks are self-reported; the report says so.

Check the reference list: DOIs, retractions, duplicates, mismatched titles. Paste, run, fix what it flags.
Citation quick check for uncited claims and overclaiming; Pattern mirror for writing patterns that weaken prose.
CiteVahti audits whether each cited source supports the exact claim: you rate first, AI second, with an exportable trail. Wording heuristics cannot do this check.

Step 6 · your report

Report

Assembled from what you ran above. Three states throughout: found/addressed, gap, needs your judgment. The last line is yours to sign; the tool has no opinion on readiness.
