## Snippet

The model does not replace your thinking; it outruns your memory of it.

## Shipped replacement

The model has outrun your memory of your own reasoning.

## Why

Semicolon-hinged reversal: deny the expected claim, substitute the clever one.
The replacement asserts the same fact in one movement, and drops "of it", which
made the reader resolve a pronoun to reach the point.
