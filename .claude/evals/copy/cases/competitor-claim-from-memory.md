## Snippet

## Shipped replacement

Sell the differentiator that survives checking: plain files you own, versus a
proprietary project database.

## Why

False. Their own help pages show one click opens the coded segments from the map.
With this audience a checkable false claim about a competitor is the fastest
trust kill available, and the differentiator that survived the check is the
stronger sell anyway. Verification means reading the competitor's current docs,
so no detector can settle it.
