## Snippet

One em-dash surviving on a page's hardest pivot, after the rest were converted
to commas, full stops and semicolons.

## Why

The rule is a budget, not a ban, and the dogfooded pass *halved* the dash count
rather than zeroing it. An edit that made every em-dash a violation would fail
this case. Judgement-only: which pivot is the hardest is a reading, and the count
is per page, not per sentence.
