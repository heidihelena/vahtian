We recruited participants at three sites in Finland between May 2019 and May 2020, with follow-up telephone interviews continuing through May 2021 for the cohort that consented to extended contact. The primary outcome was the change in FEV1 from baseline to 12 months, expressed in millilitres and analysed with a linear mixed model adjusted for age, sex, and smoking status.

The mean age was 54.3 years (SD 11.2) in the intervention arm and 55.1 years (SD 10.8) in the control arm. The between-group difference in FEV1 was 42 mL (95% CI 12 to 71). The effect was larger among never-smokers, where the difference reached 0.68 standard deviations, and the interaction term was significant at p equal to 0.003. All analyses were run in R version 4.2.1, and two-sided p values below 0.05 were treated as evidence against the null.
