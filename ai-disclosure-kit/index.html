What's inside: 10 files

  • Quick start guide PDFSet up the record, classify planned uses, and decide which entries belong in the final disclosure.
  • AI use log WordA dated record of provider, tool, model or version, access route, task, scope, use, and verification.
  • Manuscript disclosure templates WordSeven adaptable statements, from no generative AI use to a combined multi-tool record, plus cover- and revision-letter wording.
  • Thesis disclosure template WordDeclaration page and detailed appendix for master's and doctoral theses.
  • Grant AI-use statements WordPreparation disclosure, planned in-project use, and a data-protection add-on.
  • Peer-review AI-safety checklist PDFConfidentiality rules, a two-minute hidden prompt-injection screen, and reviewer disclosure wording.
  • Journal policy worksheet ExcelOne dated place for the official policy, required detail, planned tool and version, classification, and action.
  • Publisher AI-policy directory PDFLinks to 12 major policies (ICMJE, COPE, Elsevier, Springer Nature, Wiley, Taylor & Francis, Sage, PLOS, JAMA, Science, IEEE, arXiv), each fetched and read on the date shown, with two side-by-side matrices: what each policy accepts, and how detailed your disclosure must be.
  • Prompt record template MarkdownA higher-resolution session record with provider, model, software version, access route, inputs, outputs, and checks.
  • Supervisor approval form WordA signed student-supervisor agreement on approved tools, versions, access routes, limits, and review points.

The rules genuinely conflict: JAMA wants tool, version, manufacturer and dates; Taylor & Francis requires acknowledging any use; Springer Nature exempts copy-editing. The directory matrices show it in one view.

Questions

Does this guarantee my paper will be accepted, or that I'm compliant?

No. The kit helps you keep a dated record, inspect the venue's current instructions, and draft a specific disclosure. It does not certify compliance, predict acceptance, or replace legal, editorial, or institutional advice.

Do I even need to disclose AI use?

It depends on the venue, the tool, and the task. Use the directory to find the official page, then record the exact rule and date in the worksheet. The venue's current instructions decide what belongs in the submitted statement.

What formats are the files, and where do they open?

Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PDF, and Markdown (.md). They open in Word, LibreOffice, Google Docs and Sheets, Notion, and Obsidian. You download a single zip after purchase.
