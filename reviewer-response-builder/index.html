Practical questions

Does it write my responses for me?

No. It structures your comments and gives you editable starting phrases. You provide the scientific answer, real change, evidence, and location.

Do I have to use Excel?

No. The local HTML builder is the fastest path, and the Word workbook provides a document-based path. Excel is included for people who want the coverage table.

Will it work offline?

Yes. The builder is one local HTML file and makes no network requests. Word, Excel, PDF, and Markdown files remain ordinary files on your computer.

Is it tied to medicine?

No. The worked example is clinical and entirely invented. The stance, coverage, and location workflow is field-neutral.
