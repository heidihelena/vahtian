From Heidi

I build the tools my team and I need.

I’m Heidi Andersén, MD, PhD, a clinician-scientist in Finland. Vahtian grows from problems I keep meeting in my own studies: claims that need checking, decisions that need documenting, and data that cannot leave the clinic.

The problem I keep meeting

A research decision may need explaining months later. Vahtian keeps the evidence, the method, and the human choice close enough to reconstruct what happened.

AI can identify, summarise, compare, and suggest. It should not quietly become the scientific decision.

Why Vahtian exists

Software and AI make research faster. Literature searching is faster, drafting is faster, evidence review gets AI help. But the decisions that make up a study (what was assumed, coded, analysed, cited, and revised) end up scattered across files, prompts, scripts, and revisions nobody wrote down.

I built Vahtian to keep those decisions connected to their evidence, methods, and outputs, so the work stays inspectable long after it is done.

The first workflow was citation integrity: does the source you cited support the sentence you wrote? That became CiteVahti. It is the starting point, not the whole reason for the company.

Document the judgment. Don’t replace it.

The tools check support and consistency, and they keep the record. The scientific decision stays with you.

How the software is designed

  1. 1

    You decide

    AI assists; it does not decide. Researchers make the final call, every time.

  2. 2

    The record stays

    Every tool leaves a trail you can inspect, share, and re-run.

  3. 3

    Data stays local

    Work happens on your machine whenever the workflow allows. You keep your own data.

  4. 4

    Checks stay transparent

    Assessments are reproducible: you can see what was checked and why a flag was raised.

These rules outlast any feature. Interfaces change; the design philosophy stays.

March asks about September.

When a reviewer asks in March about a choice I made in September, I need the record, not my memory. The tools are built for that day.

Free to check, open to inspect

Most Vahtian software runs locally, on your own computer, whenever the workflow allows. Open-source components ship under permissive licences, primarily Apache 2.0; each repository states its own. The paid kits sell packaged workflows and templates, not access: checking your own work stays free.

Scientific conclusions belong to researchers. Vahtian documents how they were reached.
