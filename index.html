Auditable research workflows

When a reviewer asks how you did it, what will you show?

Vahtian builds local-first tools that help you record research decisions as you make them. Citations checked against their sources. Interviews coded under your review. Important decisions can be recorded with their inputs, outputs, and dates.

Human-first. AI-second. Auditable by design. Free checking tools to start. They stay free. Paid kits package the structure, documentation, and audit trail when the study is real.

CiteVahti’s audit workflow — blinded human → AI → adjudication

  1. Claim becomes a test. Each manuscript claim is checked against the source cited for it.
  2. Evidence, not topic. Does the cited source support the population, construct, comparison, outcome, direction, and strength of the claim, rather than merely discuss the same topic?
  3. You decide, blinded. The AI rates too, but its opinion stays hidden until you’ve rated. The human is the decider.
  4. Disagreements adjudicated. When human and AI differ, it routes to a person, never silently resolved.
  5. Guarded write. Any write-back to Zotero or the manuscript occurs only after preview and confirmation, with an undo record.
  6. Recorded. An append-only, hash-linked record of who rated what, what the AI advised, and what was finally written, enough to reveal later changes to the record, not to prove it complete or the science correct.

Open-source research packages — the methods engines, as Python and R

EpiNet open source The Epistemic Network toolkit, transparent graph & feature-space analysis where leakage-aware evaluation travels with every run (permutation null, calibration, community-aware splitting, contestability). A research demonstrator, not clinical decision support. Python; Apache-2.0.
RecoverLite open source Pre-data recovery tests for planned study designs — declare the estimand and analysis, simulate under a crossed grid, and classify recovery as PASS, RISK, or FAIL under the declared scenarios and thresholds before you collect data. A result about the instrument, not the real study. R; Apache-2.0.
AuditLite open source Chain-of-custody for AI-assisted analyses: an append-only, hash-chained trail of declared provenance, analytic decisions, and executions, with checks that flag when a reported result no longer follows from the current analysis. Not an AI detector. Python & R; Apache-2.0.

The trust contract

Vahtian does not decide scientific truth.

Vahtian builds local-first tools that keep research inputs, decisions, and outputs open to review. The tools support judgment; they do not establish scientific truth. What that means in practice depends on the tool:

Deterministic operations are inspectable

Parsing, calculations, checks, and exports record what was done, so a step can be re-run and read.

AI assessments remain advisory

Where AI evaluates evidence or text, its output does not silently become a scientific decision.

Consequential judgments remain accountable

The researcher or designated reviewer approves decisions that affect interpretation, inclusion, or reporting.

Open core, local-first

The open core, the free browser tools and open-source packages, is permissively licensed (primarily Apache-2.0) and runs on your laptop; each repository states its licence. We never paywall a researcher’s ability to check their own work.
