The audit workflow — blinded human → AI → adjudication

  1. Claim becomes a test. Each manuscript claim is checked against the source cited for it.
  2. Evidence, not topic. Does the cited paper actually support this claim? (PICO-level match, not keyword overlap.)
  3. You decide — blinded. The AI rates too, but its opinion stays hidden until you’ve rated. The human is the decider.
  4. Disagreements adjudicated. When human and AI differ, it routes to a person — never silently resolved.
  5. Guarded write. A reference enters your library only as the audited, undoable last step of your decision.
  6. Recorded. A hash-chained audit trail of who rated what, what the AI advised, and what was finally written.

The trust contract

Vahtian does not decide scientific truth.

It records whether a cited source supports a specific claim: who rated it, what the AI advised, how disagreements were adjudicated, and what was finally written. The judgment stays with the researcher; Vahtian keeps the evidence of how it was made.

The human decides

AI is a blinded, advisory second rater — never decisive, never silently propagated. Disagreements route to a person.

No silent writes

References enter your library only as the audited, undoable last step of a human decision. Dedupe fails closed.

The reasoning is recorded

A hash-chained audit trail, so the workflow can be reported transparently in a methods section.

Open core, local-first

The core is Apache-2.0 and runs on your laptop. We never paywall a researcher’s ability to verify their own work.
