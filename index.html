Built by someone who has to use it

I’m Heidi Andersén, MD, PhD, a clinician-scientist in Finland. I build Vahtian from the practical gaps I meet in study planning, qualitative analysis, peer review, and scientific writing.

About me and the work →

Not outside software. Vahtian is shaped inside active research practice, where a claim, codebook, or decision may need to be explained months later.

A simple working rule

You decide

AI can advise, but it does not silently become the research decision.

Keep the record

Inputs, outputs, dates, and human choices stay visible enough to inspect later.

Keep data local

Your manuscripts, interviews, and working files stay on your machine whenever the workflow allows.

State the limits

The tools check support and consistency. They do not decide scientific truth.

Open-source research packages — the methods engines, as Python and R

EpiNet open source The Epistemic Network toolkit, transparent graph & feature-space analysis where leakage-aware evaluation travels with every run (permutation null, calibration, community-aware splitting, contestability). A research demonstrator, not clinical decision support. Python; Apache-2.0.
RecoverLite open source Pre-data recovery tests for planned study designs — declare the estimand and analysis, simulate under a crossed grid, and classify recovery as PASS, RISK, or FAIL under the declared scenarios and thresholds before you collect data. A result about the instrument, not the real study. R; Apache-2.0.
AssessLite open source · R + Python Stress-test the assumptions behind a causal result across clusters, time, subgroups, confounding, DAG adjustment, and overlap. Export an audit record of what held, failed, or could not be resolved. Apache-2.0.
AuditLite open source Chain-of-custody for AI-assisted analyses: an append-only, hash-chained trail of declared provenance, analytic decisions, and executions, with checks that flag when a reported result no longer follows from the current analysis. Not an AI detector. Python & R; Apache-2.0.

The promise

The tool helps. You remain responsible.

Vahtian keeps inputs, outputs, and decisions open to review. It can check, compare, structure, and suggest. It does not establish scientific truth or make consequential research decisions for you.

Deterministic operations are inspectable

Parsing, calculations, checks, and exports record what was done, so a step can be re-run and read.

AI assessments remain advisory

Where AI evaluates evidence or text, its output does not silently become a scientific decision.

Consequential judgments remain accountable

The researcher or designated reviewer approves decisions that affect interpretation, inclusion, or reporting.

Open core, local-first

The open core, the free browser tools and open-source packages, is permissively licensed (primarily Apache-2.0) and runs on your laptop; each repository states its licence. We never paywall a researcher’s ability to check their own work.
