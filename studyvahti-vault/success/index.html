The vault is yours

Start with the demo, not your data.

Polar sends the download link and receipt by email. The ZIP contains the complete vault, scripts, prompts, fabricated cohort, and quickstart.

Your first hour

There is no need to install every component before you understand the product.

01
Open the vault

Unzip the download. In Obsidian, choose Open folder as vault and select StudyVahti_vault.

02
Follow the fabricated study

Open 00_Start_Here/Start Here.md, then take the guided tour from question to linked manuscript claim.

03
Check your setup

Run python3 studyvahti.py doctor. It tells you what is present and what to add for checks, figures, and the optional assistant.

04
Create a working copy

Use one copy per study. Confirm your ethics, data-management, path, backup, and sync settings before linking sensitive material.

When something does not run

Email hello@vahtian.com with the command you ran and the full output. Keep the Polar receipt; it also carries the download and your 1.x updates.
