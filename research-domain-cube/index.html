Start with the work already on your desk

A research plan often begins as a pile: one promising dataset, two unfinished papers, a grant idea, and several questions that may or may not be new. The cube gives that pile a shape you can inspect with a supervisor, collaborator or team.

01 / MAP

Build the field

Write one core thesis, three useful axes and nine zones. Split the zones into 27 concrete project moves.

02 / AUDIT

Mark what supports each cell

Separate evidence from assumptions, searches still to run, and gaps verified through a documented search.

03 / CHALLENGE

Compare the candidates

Score importance and feasibility. Then read the sources, question the scores and make the decision yourself.

What comes out of it

Not a verdict. A short list worth discussing.

Paper candidate

A useful project with evidence in hand and a realistic route to completion.

Grant candidate

An important project that needs people, access, data or time you do not yet have.

Gap candidate to verify

A question that remained open after a named search. It is still a candidate, not proof that nobody has done the work.

Where the kit stops

It is not a literature review

The map helps you decide where a review or search is needed. It does not replace one.

It does not establish a gap

An empty cell may mean the axis is weak, the search is unfinished or the question is poorly framed.

It does not choose the project

The scoring narrows the field. You remain responsible for scientific value, feasibility and consequences.

Questions

Do I need AI to use it?

No. The workbook, planner and cube builder work without AI. The prompt pack is there when a skeptical second reader would help.

Can AI fill the cube for me?

It can suggest wording, counterexamples and searches. It cannot supply your scientific judgment, verify a source or establish a field gap.

Does it only work in medicine?

No. The structure is field-neutral. The worked example is clinical because that is the research practice the kit came from.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time EUR 29 digital download.
