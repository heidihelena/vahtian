Agile writing · Starting point

What is actually keeping your paper from getting finished?

This check does not measure motivation, talent, or whether you are a good writer. It shows where your work is strong and where it is stuck.

16 statements · about 4 minutes · scored in your browser

I just need more motivation.The work is stuck on one decision.

Rate each statement by how well it describes your situation right now. The result is computed in your own browser.

Scale: 1 = not at all, 2 = a little, 3 = partly, 4 = fairly well, 5 = completely.
