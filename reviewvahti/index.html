2 · Per-claim reliability

κ is shown with raw % agreement, PABAK, and AC1 — never alone. Screening skews to one category, so κ can read low even at high agreement (the “kappa paradox”); the adjacent measures keep it honest. The AI second opinion is never a ballot.

ReviewVahti · static, in-browser, no telemetry, no outbound calls · reads only the files you load. Reliability is measured over human reviewers; the AI second opinion is advisory and never counts toward κ.
